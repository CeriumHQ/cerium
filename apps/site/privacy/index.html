Privacy

Cerium has no telemetry. Your threads live in a database on your machine, and each harness talks to its own provider as it does without Cerium. This page covers what Cerium itself keeps or reaches out for.

Your Cerium account

Signing in is optional. It lets your phone and other machines follow your threads. Sign-in runs on Clerk, with an email and password or with Google, GitHub or X, which share your name, email address and profile picture with Clerk. Cerium never sees your password.

Once you're signed in, Cerium's relay at cerium.nonlooped.xyz keeps, for your account:

  • your name, email address and profile picture from the sign-in;
  • each device's name, platform, Cerium version and update channel, when it was last seen, and a hash of its device key;
  • a copy of every thread on your signed-in machines: its messages, tool calls and their output, without images;
  • what you're drafting and when each thread was last read;
  • your browsers' push subscriptions, if you turn notifications on.

While you use Cerium from another device, the relay also passes traffic between it and your machine. The relay runs on Cloudflare, which keeps its request logs. None of it is sold or shared for advertising.

Removing it

Signing a machine out, or removing it in Settings → Account, deletes its threads' copies from the relay, and deleting a thread deletes its copy. To have your whole account deleted, open an issue on GitHub from the address you signed in with, or ask through it for a private way to reach us.

What the app reaches out for

  • Updates. The app checks Cerium's GitHub Releases at launch and every six hours, and downloads an update only when you ask. Settings → Harnesses looks up each harness's newest version on npm or GitHub.
  • Dictation. The speech model you pick downloads from Hugging Face on first use, and your voice is transcribed on your machine.
  • Artifacts. Pages an agent draws can load scripts over HTTPS, such as chart libraries from cdn.jsdelivr.net, so viewing one may fetch from that CDN.

This website

The website runs no analytics or ads. Signing in sets Clerk's cookies, which keep you signed in. Cloudflare serves the site and keeps request logs.
